## ⏱️ 60-minute class flow

| Minutes | Segment | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **0–2** | **Intro + brand claim** | Show game page. Students form 6 groups, open page on one laptop per group, click their group tab (G1–G6), claim a brand. First-come first-served. |
| **2–12** | **Lecture block 1** (slides 1–7) | Cover: agenda, marketing management framework, mission/vision, demand generation vs. harvesting, the funnel (Attention → Interest → Desire → Action). |
| **12–20** | **🎮 Round 1 · Spot the Funnel** (8 min) | Students open Round 1 on their group page. Walk around. At minute 18, call out "2 minutes left." At 20, ask 2–3 groups to share their ad rewrite. |
| **20–30** | **Lecture block 2** (slides 8–15) | Cover: Digital SWOT, STP (segmentation/targeting/positioning), value proposition example (Peloton), resource allocation, channel comparison table (slide 13). |
| **30–42** | **🎮 Round 2 · Budget Wars** (12 min) | At minute 30, they start allocating. At minute **36**, announce the PLOT TWIST: "Meta just raised ad costs 30% AND your email provider had a breach — email is paused. Reallocate with $7,000 and no email." At minute 40, ask 2 groups to pitch. |
| **42–52** | **Lecture block 3** (slides 16–25) | Cover: 4Ps digital strategies, pricing models (subscription/flat/bundled/freemium), 7 A's framework, privacy/security/legal/ethical considerations. |
| **52–58** | **🎮 Round 3 · Crisis Mode** (6 min) | Each group reads their assigned crisis (auto-shown based on their brand) and drafts the 3-step plan. Walk around and peek at the instructor dashboard. |
| **58–60** | **Wrap + exam preview** | Slides 26–33. Crown the group with the most thoughtful crisis response. Remind them about the exam at the DLC. Dismiss. |

## 🎯 Round-by-round facilitation notes

### Round 1 · Spot the Funnel (8 min)

**The point:** Students must diagnose whether their brand needs demand GENERATION (awareness) or HARVESTING (conversion) based on the Key Challenge in their brief. Then they pick an ad that matches and rewrite it.

**Good answers to reward:**
- BarkBox Deluxe, NapPods, StudyBrew → likely need GENERATION (people don't know them yet)
- ChiliMail, ReRun, GrandmaBot → mixed/harvesting (existing interest, conversion friction)
- The rewrite should specify all 4 elements: channel, hook, audience, CTA

**Common mistake:** Students pick an ad they personally like instead of one that matches the strategy.

**Share out:** Ask 2 groups to read their rewrite aloud.

---

### Round 2 · Budget Wars (12 min) ⭐ CENTERPIECE

**The point:** Force channel-selection thinking using the cost/concentration/effectiveness table from slide 13.

**The plot twist is the lesson:** Announce it at the 6-minute mark: *"Meta just raised ad costs 30% AND your email provider had a breach — email is paused. You have $7,000 left and no email. Reallocate. 3 minutes."*

**Good answers to reward:**
- Total adds to exactly $10,000 initially
- Justification per channel ties to the customer (e.g. "Grandmabot puts 60% on Facebook Ads because the buyer is the adult child, not the user")
- Plot twist reallocation names what they CUT and why (not just where they added)

**Share out:** Pick 2 groups with contrasting allocations (e.g., SEO-heavy vs. Paid Search-heavy) to pitch.

---

### Round 3 · Crisis Mode (6 min)

**The point:** Apply privacy/legal/ethical principles to a brand-specific crisis. Each brand gets a tailored crisis (see below).

**Crisis scenarios (pre-assigned by brand):**

| Brand | Crisis type | Key principle |
|---|---|---|
| BarkBox Deluxe | Product safety (dogs sick from treats, one died) | Recall process, consumer trust, transparency |
| NapPods | Privacy breach (unencrypted camera footage public) | Data protection, incident response |
| GrandmaBot | Liability (AI advised senior who was scammed) | Vulnerable population, AI ethics, disclosure |
| ReRun Sneakers | Authenticity scandal (12% fake products) | Consumer trust, refund policy, vendor accountability |
| ChiliMail | Cultural appropriation (fake Mexican branding by white founder) | Brand partnership vetting, cultural sensitivity |
| StudyBrew | Product safety (student caffeine overdose) | Product labeling, warnings, retailer relations |

**Good answers have:**
- **Step 1 (immediate, first 24 hours):** STOP the harmful thing, notify legal, issue holding statement
- **Step 2 (public comm):** Specific channel, tone, and WHO the message is addressed to (not just "we'll post on social")
- **Step 3 (long-term):** Actual structural fix — new policy, supplier audits, warning labels — not just "we'll do better"

## 🏆 Who wins?

There's no points system. At the end, you can informally recognize:
- **Most creative rewrite** in Round 1
- **Sharpest pitch** in Round 2
- **Most thorough crisis plan** in Round 3

Or pick ONE overall winner based on the group that consistently showed strong strategic thinking. Candy to the winners.

## 📊 Grading (if collecting for participation)

Since answers save to the browser, students should either:
- Take screenshots of their filled-in game page and post to Canvas, OR
- Stay on the page and let you walk around checking at the end

**Suggested 10-point rubric:**
- 3 pts — Round 1 complete (all 3 fields filled, strategy matches brand need)
- 4 pts — Round 2 complete (budget sums to $10k, justifications tied to brand, plot twist reallocation done)
- 3 pts — Round 3 complete (all 3 steps of crisis plan drafted)

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

**"Students accidentally claimed the wrong brand"**
→ Click the "Change brand" button on the brief card. Releases it for others.

**"Two groups clicked the same brand at the same time"**
→ Whoever clicked last wins (their claim overwrites). Have one group pick a different brand.

**"Answers disappeared!"**
→ Likely a browser refresh cleared memory, OR they're on a different device. Ask the same laptop to stay on the tab throughout class.

**"The Instructor tab doesn't show what my students wrote"**
→ The Instructor view only sees data saved on YOUR laptop. Students' answers are on THEIR laptops. To view everyone's work: walk around, or project a single group's tab.

**"Class is running over"**
→ Cut the Round 3 sharing. Just have them fill it in, don't do a share-out. Recovers 2 min.
